Send a Good Day ☀

About this project

We believe a good day is something you can give someone. Not a big gesture — just a name, a warm message, and thirty seconds. So we built a free way to send one: pick a moment, pick a message, and send it to someone who could use it today.

Send a Good Day is made by Good Day Coffee, a woman-owned, small-batch coffee roaster in St. Louis Park, Minnesota. We roast coffee that does a little good every day — 100% of proceeds from our North Star Blend support local causes we believe in. This project is another way of doing that: no purchase required, just a little kindness passed along.

If a card led you here and you'd like to try the coffee, there's 10% off waiting for you. And if you'd like to send your own good day, it takes about thirty seconds.
